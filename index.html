Skills-to-Career Alignment Tool

Connect evidence from your coursework and academic projects to skills employers request.

How to use this tool

Describe one educational experience and what you personally did. Then enter skills or requirements from a job description. The tool will look for possible connections between your evidence and those employer-requested skills.

Important: A possible match does not prove that you possess a skill. Review each result and decide whether your experience provides enough evidence to support the connection.
